About Us

Furniture chosen for real rooms, not just photos.

What we do

Making every detail come together.

FURNIQOS works with homeowners, architects and interior designers to source furniture and interior solutions directly from manufacturers in China. We manage the process beyond selection, including procurement, quality checks, logistics and installation.

Whether it’s a home, a villa or a commercial space, we bring the products and people together to help turn your plans into a finished space.

Our story

Built around the way
great spaces come together.

A beautiful interior is about more than what you see. It is also about finding the right products, getting the details right and making sure everything comes together as planned.

With over 27 years of architecture and interior project experience, we understand what it takes to move from an idea to a finished space. FURNIQOS brings sourcing, procurement and execution under one roof, making the process easier to manage from start to finish.
