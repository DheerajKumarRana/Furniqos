A contemporary retreat where warm materials, natural light, and architectural simplicity create an atmosphere of effortless luxury.

Project
Private Residence
Client
Private family
Date
January 2026
Budget

Overview

This project furnished the open living and dining area of a three-bedroom apartment. FURNIQOS supplied a three-seater and two-seater sofa set, a six-seater dining table with chairs, and a low storage unit, all sourced from our manufacturing partners in China and delivered ready to place.

The Problem

The client wanted solid, good-looking furniture but found local showroom prices for that quality too high. They also worried about buying imported pieces without seeing them first, and the living and dining area was narrow, so oversized pieces would not fit.

The Solution

We picked sofas and a dining table in sizes that matched the floor plan, and shared exact dimensions before ordering. Every piece went through our quality check at the factory and a second check after arriving in India, with photos sent to the client at each stage, so they could see what they were getting before it reached their home.

Project Outcome

The family now has a living and dining space that fits the room, feels considered and was delivered on budget. Each piece arrived checked and ready to place, and the savings from buying direct went into the quality of the furniture rather than into retail margins.
